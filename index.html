The institute

FAIR advances the fundamental science of AI—unifying learning, reasoning, perception, and language into frameworks that are transparent, scalable, and human-aligned.

Rooted in the breadth of UT Dallas, FAIR is an umbrella institute spanning learning, reasoning, knowledge representation, planning, and language—the whole of AI, not one corner of it. Founding centers and partner institutes keep their own identity while sharing a single research agenda.

Why fundamental AI

Weak foundations
propagate everywhere.

Today’s systems hallucinate, fail silently under distribution shift, cannot explain their conclusions, and offer no formal guarantees of safety. These are not application-level bugs to be patched one deployment at a time—they are symptoms of missing science.

A hospital cannot certify a model it cannot verify. A regulator cannot audit a decision process with no interpretable structure. Strengthen the foundations and every system built on them improves at once.

Research

Thirteen thrusts.
One shared agenda.

From tractable inference to multimodal reasoning, FAIR’s agenda spans the full arc of intelligent behavior—learning, reasoning, perception, language, and action.

Tractable and Certifiable Inference

Probabilistic circuits, structure-exploiting inference, and algorithms with provable convergence guarantees.

Neuro-Symbolic Learning with Human Guidance

Interactive systems that incorporate human preferences, corrections, and feedback in transparent ways.

Sriraam Natarajan

Multimodal Scene Understanding

Robust and Trustworthy AI

Models and verification methods that resist adversarial attacks and remain fair under distribution shifts.

Feng Chen
